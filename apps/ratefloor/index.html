Why salary divided by 2,080 hours can mislead freelancers

Employees often have paid leave, benefits and a predictable salary. Freelancers spend time selling, writing proposals, doing administration and waiting for feedback. Only part of their working time can be billed to clients. A realistic freelance hourly rate calculation must account for nonbillable hours, time off, overhead and a reserve for taxes.

This free rate floor calculator works backward from your own target income. Use the sensitivity table to see how lower billable utilization changes the required rate. The result is a business sustainability estimate, not a promise that customers will accept your quote.
