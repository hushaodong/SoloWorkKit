What is a construction change order?

A change order documents work that was not included in the original agreement. The most useful request tells the client what changed, the estimated additional cost, the expected effect on the schedule and whether the work is approved. This free tool separates direct cost, overhead and target gross margin so small contractors can see how a quote was calculated.
