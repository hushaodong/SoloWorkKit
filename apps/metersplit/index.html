Understand the difference between two shared-meter allocations

A single water meter may serve two rental units even when the number of occupants differs. MeterSplit shows two hypothetical approaches: equal amounts per unit, and a hybrid that splits fixed base charges equally while allocating the remaining bill in proportion to occupant-days. It does not claim that either method is fair, lawful or an estimate of actual consumption.

Enter the bill period, total and fixed charges, then add each unit's residents and days. Review both columns and export a CSV to discuss the assumptions. Actual utility usage varies by behavior and fixtures, and many jurisdictions require submetering, specific lease language or landlord-paid utilities. Consult your lease and local rules before collecting any amount.
