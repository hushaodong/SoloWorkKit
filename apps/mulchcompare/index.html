What this calculator helps you decide

Buying mulch by the bag can be convenient for a small garden, while bulk delivery may become cheaper as volume grows. MulchCompare converts each rectangular bed from square feet and depth to cubic feet, then to cubic yards. It rounds bags and bulk quantities to your selected purchase units.

Compare the full delivered cost rather than only the price per cubic yard. Product coverage varies with mulch texture, compaction and depth; measure irregular beds in smaller rectangles for a closer estimate.
