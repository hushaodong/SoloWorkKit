Turn lease dates into your own calendar events

Independent landlords may track lease expirations in spreadsheets and calendar notes. LeaseTick converts each entered lease end date and your chosen lead times into planning and notice-review dates, then exports a standard iCalendar file.

Lead times are user supplied because notice requirements vary by jurisdiction, lease and situation. Always verify legal requirements and actual notice delivery separately. Downloaded events are not automatic alerts until imported into a calendar that supports reminders.
