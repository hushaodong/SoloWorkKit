Not a legal agreement, rights clearance or market valuation. Confirm copyright ownership, releases, permitted media, term, territory and exclusivity in a reviewed contract.

Why spell out photography usage rights?

A commercial photo shoot fee and the right to reuse photographs are different parts of a client engagement. Ambiguity often appears when an image originally intended for a website is later reused in paid advertising, print brochures or by another business. A clear license scope records the agreed channels, territory, term and exclusivity before delivery.

This worksheet creates a client-readable summary and an editable illustrative quote. It is not a stock photography rate database: the multipliers are examples to help compare scenarios, not a claim about what a buyer should pay. Export a scope draft and use your own written license agreement for any actual transfer of rights.
