Estimate photo backup storage before buying another drive

A photography archive grows differently from a phone photo library: RAW files, edited exports, video and redundant copies add up. PhotoVaultPlan forecasts yearly unique storage and then separately estimates how many physical drives are needed for a user-chosen number of local copies.

What this forecast does not do

The cloud estimate is a storage-only hypothetical based on the rate you enter. It excludes transfer charges, minimum billing, retention policies, drive replacement, NAS enclosures and restore costs. Unlimited-computer backup products and object storage have different rules; compare the vendor plan before purchasing. Choosing a file sample never uploads the files.

Use a 3-2-1-style backup plan as a starting point, and verify recoverability with restore tests. RAID redundancy alone does not protect against accidental deletion or site loss.
