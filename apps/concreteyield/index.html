Calculate concrete bags and cubic yards for several pours

Contractors and DIY builders may know slab dimensions but struggle to reconcile bag counts, total weight and ready-mix volume. ConcreteYield sums rectangular slabs and round post holes separately, applies an explicit overage, then converts cubic feet to cubic yards and estimated whole bags.

How the math works

Rectangular volume = length × width × thickness in feet. Round-hole volume = π × radius² × depth, multiplied by hole count. Divide the volume including your chosen allowance by the product-specific bag yield and round up. Example yields of 0.60 ft³ for an 80 lb bag and 0.45 ft³ for a 60 lb bag are approximate; the package is authoritative.
