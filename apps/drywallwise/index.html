Estimate drywall sheets room by room

Drywall takeoffs are easy to overstate when doors, windows, ceiling coverage and sheet dimensions are mixed together. Add rooms separately and subtract measured opening areas. The calculator converts net square footage to whole sheets with an explicit overage allowance.

The result is an area-based planning quantity, not a cut optimization or a construction specification. Compare the total with the actual panel layout and installer requirements.
