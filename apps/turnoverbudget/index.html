What this calculator helps you decide

Between tenants, owners may spend money on cleaning, painting, rekeying, repairs and utilities while also losing rent during vacancy. TurnoverBudget separates these owner costs from tenant security-deposit deductions. It uses a simple 30-day monthly-rent convention to estimate vacancy loss, not a legally required proration formula.

Compare expected costs with actual expenses, then export a record for your property budget. The tool stores expense rows only on your device and does not automatically save receipts or photos.

Can I charge all turnover expenses to the former tenant?

No. This is a property-owner budget. Whether a specific cost can be charged to a tenant depends on the lease, local law, ordinary wear and supporting evidence.
