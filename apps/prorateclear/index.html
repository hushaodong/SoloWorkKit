Why two landlords can calculate different partial-month rents

Rent proration commonly divides monthly rent by the number of calendar days in the month or by a fixed 30-day convention. For example, a 31-day month with $2,400 monthly rent has a daily rate of $77.42 using actual days, versus $80 using a 30-day divisor. Neither formula is universally correct for every lease and jurisdiction.
