Why cleaning quotes need more than square footage

A whole-home square-foot estimate can overcharge a customer who wants only the kitchen and bathrooms, or underprice a job with unusually intensive cleaning. A room-based cleaning quote starts with the areas included and a realistic estimate of labor hours for each area. It then adds supplies, travel, overhead and a margin for the business.

This calculator lets cleaning owners add, remove and edit rooms or tasks before quoting. It shows the per-visit amount and a monthly equivalent for recurring work. Every assumption can be changed; the result is a proposal draft, not a binding price or a regional market rate.
