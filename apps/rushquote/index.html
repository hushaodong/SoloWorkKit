Make rush work an explicit pricing decision

Freelancers often accept last-minute changes without considering overtime, displaced client work or the practical cost of a compressed deadline. RushQuote shows two alternative methods: a percentage premium on the original project fee, and a cost-based premium for additional out-of-hours work at a chosen margin.

The larger number is displayed for comparison, not as a mandated charge. You still need to confirm whether the deadline is realistic and whether the original contract permits an expedited fee. Use the copyable quote to propose a specific amount, turnaround and approval condition. For recurring retainers, agree on an urgent-request policy before the next emergency.
