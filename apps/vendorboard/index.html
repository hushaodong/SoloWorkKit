Keep property repairs and vendors in one place

Small landlords often coordinate multiple trades across different properties with a spreadsheet and phone contacts. VendorBoard is a focused job assignment log: enter the property, vendor, trade, next follow-up and status, then export a portable CSV.

It intentionally avoids tenant records, payment details and shared portals. Dates are for manual follow-up; this MVP does not send notifications or manage vendor credentials.
