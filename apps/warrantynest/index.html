Know which rental appliance warranty may still apply

When a refrigerator or HVAC component fails, a landlord may need the model, serial number, installation date and warranty documents before contacting a service technician. WarrantyNest keeps a simple appliance-by-property inventory in this browser and calculates an estimated expiration date from the purchase date and warranty term you enter.

Export a CSV for independent backup or an ICS calendar file for manual import into your preferred calendar. Calendar events are reminders to check coverage, not proof of warranty validity. Manufacturers may have different coverage for parts and labor or require timely registration. This MVP does not upload receipts, sync across devices or send notifications. Keep the original documents somewhere safe.
